## File Naming Conventions

### Review Files (`docs/reviews/`)
- **Format:** `[scope]-r[round]-YYYY-MM-DD-[HHMM].md`
- **Example:** `frontend-r1-2026-04-23-1644.md`
- **Components:**
  - `scope` = frontend, architecture, testing, bpmn, etc.
  - `r[round]` = round number (r1, r2, r3, etc.)
  - `YYYY-MM-DD` = date
  - `HHMM` = start time (24-hour format, no colon)
