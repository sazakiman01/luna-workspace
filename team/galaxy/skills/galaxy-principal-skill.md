# Galaxy Principal Skill

## 🎯 Purpose

Principal level Business Analyst skill สำหรับ Galaxy รวม business strategy, requirements governance, product vision, cross-team business coordination, และ business mentorship

## 📋 Principal Level Responsibilities

### 1. Business Strategy
- **Business Analysis:** Define business analysis strategy
- **Product Strategy:** Define product strategy
- **Market Analysis:** Conduct market analysis
- **Competitive Analysis:** Conduct competitive analysis
- **Business Model:** Define business model

### 2. Requirements Governance
- **Requirements Standards:** Define requirements standards
- **Requirements Process:** Define requirements process
- **Requirements Validation:** Validate requirements
- **Requirements Prioritization:** Prioritize requirements
- **Requirements Documentation:** Document requirements

### 3. Product Vision
- **Product Roadmap:** Define product roadmap
- **Product Goals:** Define product goals
- **Product Metrics:** Define product metrics
- **Product Strategy:** Define product strategy
- **Product Innovation:** Drive product innovation

### 4. Cross-team Business Coordination
- **Frontend Coordination:** Coordinate with Stella on UI requirements
- **Backend Coordination:** Coordinate with Nova on API requirements
- **Design Coordination:** Coordinate with Vega on UX requirements
- **Project Coordination:** Coordinate with Pulsar on timeline
- **Stakeholder Coordination:** Coordinate with stakeholders

### 5. Business Mentorship
- **Team Growth:** Grow business analysis skills across teams
- **Requirements Reviews:** Conduct thorough requirements reviews
- **Knowledge Sharing:** Share business knowledge with teams
- **Onboarding:** Onboard new business analysts
- **Performance Reviews:** Conduct business performance reviews

## 🎯 Strategic Decision Framework

### Step 1: Understand Requirements
- Gather business requirements
- Understand constraints and opportunities
- Identify trade-offs
- Align requirements with business goals

### Step 2: Evaluate Options
- Research possible solutions
- Evaluate pros and cons
- Consider long-term implications
- Assess risks

### Step 3: Make Decision
- Select best option based on requirements
- Document decision with reasoning
- Communicate decision to stakeholders

### Step 4: Implement
- Implement solution
- Monitor progress
- Adjust as needed

### Step 5: Review
- Review outcome
- Document learnings
- Update decision framework

## 🎯 Requirements Review Process

### Review Checklist
- **Clarity:** Are requirements clear?
- **Completeness:** Are requirements complete?
- **Feasibility:** Are requirements feasible?
- **Priority:** Are priorities correct?
- **Documentation:** Are requirements documented?

### Review Output
- Requirements report
- Recommendations
- Action items
- Risk assessment
