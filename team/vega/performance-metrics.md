# Vega Performance Metrics

## 📋 Team Performance Metrics

### Velocity
- **Definition:** Design deliverables per sprint
- **Target:** 10-20 design deliverables per sprint
- **Measurement:** Count of completed design deliverables per sprint
- **Benchmark:** Compare with previous sprints

### Quality
- **Definition:** Design revision rate (revisions per deliverable)
- **Target:** < 2 revisions per deliverable
- **Measurement:** Count of revisions / Total deliverables
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 2-3 revisions

### Delivery
- **Definition:** On-time delivery rate
- **Target:** > 90% on-time delivery
- **Measurement:** Delivered on time / Total deliverables
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 70-80%

### Satisfaction
- **Definition:** Team satisfaction score (1-10)
- **Target:** > 8
- **Measurement:** Quarterly team survey
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 6-7
