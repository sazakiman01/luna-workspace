## 🎯 Strategic Decision Framework

### Step 1: Understand Requirements
- Gather audit requirements
- Understand constraints and opportunities
- Identify trade-offs
- Align audit with business goals

### Step 2: Evaluate Options
- Research possible solutions
- Evaluate pros and cons
- Consider long-term implications
- Assess risks

### Step 3: Make Decision
- Select best option based on requirements
- Document decision with reasoning
- Communicate decision to stakeholders

### Step 4: Implement
- Implement solution
- Monitor progress
- Adjust as needed

### Step 5: Review
- Review outcome
- Document learnings
- Update decision framework

## 🎯 Audit Review Process

### Review Checklist
- **Completeness:** Is audit complete?
- **Accuracy:** Is audit accurate?
- **Timeliness:** Is audit timely?
- **Actionability:** Are recommendations actionable?
- **Documentation:** Is audit documented?

### Review Output
- Audit report
- Recommendations
- Action items
- Risk assessment
