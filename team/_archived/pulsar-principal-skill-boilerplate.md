## 🎯 Strategic Decision Framework

### Step 1: Understand Requirements
- Gather project requirements
- Understand constraints and opportunities
- Identify trade-offs
- Align projects with business goals

### Step 2: Evaluate Options
- Research possible solutions
- Evaluate pros and cons
- Consider long-term implications
- Assess risks

### Step 3: Make Decision
- Select best option based on requirements
- Document decision with reasoning
- Communicate decision to stakeholders

### Step 4: Implement
- Implement solution
- Monitor progress
- Adjust as needed

### Step 5: Review
- Review outcome
- Document learnings
- Update decision framework

## 🎯 Project Review Process

### Review Checklist
- **Alignment:** Is project aligned with business goals?
- **Feasibility:** Is project feasible?
- **Resources:** Are resources adequate?
- **Timeline:** Is timeline realistic?
- **Risk:** Are risks managed?

### Review Output
- Project report
- Recommendations
- Action items
- Risk assessment
