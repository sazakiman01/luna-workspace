# Stella Performance Metrics

## 📋 Team Performance Metrics

### Velocity
- **Definition:** Story points completed per sprint
- **Target:** 50-100 story points per sprint
- **Measurement:** Sum of completed story points per sprint
- **Benchmark:** Compare with previous sprints

### Quality
- **Definition:** Bug rate (bugs per story point)
- **Target:** < 0.1 bugs per story point
- **Measurement:** Count of bugs / Total story points
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 0.2-0.5

### Delivery
- **Definition:** On-time delivery rate
- **Target:** > 90% on-time delivery
- **Measurement:** Delivered on time / Total deliverables
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 70-80%

### Satisfaction
- **Definition:** Team satisfaction score (1-10)
- **Target:** > 8
- **Measurement:** Quarterly team survey
- **Benchmark:** Industry average is 6-7
